# Acknowledgements

LV LEDA is a research-oriented catalogue and atlas for galaxies of the nearby Universe. Its development is associated with the [Special Astrophysical Observatory of the Russian Academy of Sciences](https://www.sao.ru/) and the wider [LEDA](https://leda.sao.ru/) data infrastructure.

## Institutions and scientific resources

We acknowledge the published observational studies and catalogue work on which the nearby-galaxy data depend. Scientific credit for individual measurements belongs to their original sources.

Source catalogue: [_Updated Nearby Galaxy Catalog_ — Karachentsev, Makarov & Kaisina (2013)](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-6256/145/4/101).

Additional contributor, infrastructure and funding acknowledgements will be added only after the relevant names and grant details have been verified.

For bibliographic records of research using LV LEDA, see [Publications](/publications).
