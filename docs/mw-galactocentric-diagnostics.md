# MW.4b Galactocentric diagnostics

The MW.3 formula epoch and tidal calibration remain unchanged. For a unique
MW root with usable adopted coordinates and distance, use exact heliocentric
vector differences in ICRS, not a projected external-host approximation.
The sample excludes the root, unconfirmed members, nonindividual distances,
untraceable lineage and review statuses other than VERIFIED or NOT_REVIEWED.
The centre is nominal and not required to pass an extragalactic review gate.
All radii are nominal, not independently verified or completeness corrected.

New diagnostics: cumulative Galactocentric radius counts, morphology versus
Galactocentric radius, and HI/M26 versus Galactocentric radius (upper limits
retained). CSV exports carry PGC, nominal heliocentric and MW centre geometry,
paired method/reference and review status. Counts, maximum, mean and median
radii in Group Properties refer only to this selected sample. No radius is
interpreted as a halo boundary. No new velocity transformation, mass estimator,
crossing time, uncertainty propagation or database write is introduced.
External groups retain their existing diagnostics unchanged.
