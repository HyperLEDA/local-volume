# Stage 07j.5 — Geometric Galactic latitude guide

Appearance → Reference guides → Zone of Avoidance (geometric). Off by default, 10 degrees illustrative default, range 1–30 degrees. Two translucent conical boundaries at Galactic b = ±b_lim, with ring and radial lines truncated at current scene reference extent. The region between the cones is the angular band; it is not an observed obscuration boundary or survey completeness model. Truncation radius is display-only. The same sky directions are valid in all three coordinate systems and in Real / Real matched / Redshift because radial remapping retains sky direction. No galaxy filter, change in density estimator, extinction correction, selection correction, or catalogue mutation. Group aggregates may overlap the illustrative band and do not establish individual member coverage. Session and Reset supported; guide does not enter picking or camera identity. Clean PNG captures the guide and includes the latitude limit in its filename. Actual survey coverage, extinction and depth maps are separate work under #80, requiring source/version and independent geometry tests. Ref #108. Browser acceptance and full project QA required.
