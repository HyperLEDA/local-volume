# Stage 07j.2b — Local density mapping

Colour by Local density in Appearance explicitly switches to individual galaxies. Fixed-radius N/(4πR³/3) and kth-neighbour k/(4πr_k³/3) are nominal observed-sample estimators. The target is excluded, duplicate identities rejected, zero fixed-radius counts valid, kNN insufficient neighbours or zero support volume unavailable. k is fixed even for tied distances. No bias, completeness, survey-mask or distance-error correction. The reference sample is identical to spatial environment, before slice/population filters, after volume and distance-quality filters. Only in the 11 Mpc view, support spheres crossing its boundary are flagged and omitted by default; inclusion is explicit and uncorrected. Other catalogue boundaries and holes are not corrected or inferred. Linear colour scale; missing values neutral. Group markers are not density estimates. Changing estimator does not alter geometric selection or camera; changing from group mode uses the existing display-mode transition. Density settings are session-local and resettable. PNG remains a clean image without the legend. Scientific acceptance requires real catalogue comparisons, masks/selection, uncertainty and browser QA. No database/backend changes.
