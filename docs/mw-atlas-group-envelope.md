# MW.4c: observer-centred Atlas group envelope

The observer remains at the Solar position. MW is excluded from the ordinary
extragalactic galaxy sample, distance intervals, matched redshift geometry,
nominal density and connectivity algorithms. Only Physical Groups mode gains
a dedicated selectable bubble, centred on the adopted MW centre. Its radius
is the maximum nominal Galactocentric separation among unique canonical
satellites with REVIEW_READY individual distances and VERIFIED or NOT_REVIEWED
value review. Both centre and member distances must match the group's formula
version and the member revision must match the centre's computed revision.
The full qualified member sample defines the radius independently of the
current volume cut. The centre must agree with the group summary. No projected
extent is used. No qualified members or incompatible geometry means no bubble.
The MW bubble is absent in verified-only mode and is not a halo boundary.
The inspector reports its qualified sample count; ordinary groups are unchanged.
MW retains canonical group navigation and separate focus scale. No database
changes or scientific sample changes are introduced. The numeric galaxy colour
scale still excludes MW, so numeric colours may be unavailable for this bubble.
