# Stage 07j.4 — Matched real / redshift geometry

View → Geometry: Real (original view), Real matched, Redshift. The two matched modes use identical PGCs with eligible individual catalogue D and finite positive catalogue VLG, at shared H0. Redshift positions are observer-direction unit vectors multiplied by VLG/H0; not barycentre-corrected positions, independent measurements, or an inferred 3D velocity field. Nonpositive velocities are excluded, never mirrored or placed at the origin. The physical distance/volume, region and population filters remain real-space filters; actual redshift radii can exceed 11 Mpc. Matched geometry uses a common extent and camera key based on both sets of positions. The current camera may refit when entering the matched pair or changing H0; switching within the pair uses the same camera identity. Colours retain real-space semantics. In both matched modes group aggregates, physical shells, region outlines, distance intervals, neighbourhood and MD/velocity arrow layers are paused; physical sample transfer is disabled until returning to Real. Plane/grid are geometric reference axes. Inspector retains real coordinates and separately displays velocity proxy radius. Hidden objects remain available via catalogue search subject to real filters. PNG remains clean and filenames identify matched space and H0. No database or backend changes; no uncertainty propagation, selection correction, or claim of physical distances from velocity. Refs #105 #108. Full project QA and real browser acceptance required.
