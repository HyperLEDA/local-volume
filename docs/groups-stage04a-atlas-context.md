# Stage 04a — Atlas group context (#64)

Atlas click resolves only a unique accepted physical membership. MD suite and published definitions are not automatically substituted. Conflicting physical identities are treated as unavailable; the individual MD fallback remains distinct. The standalone context panel and chooser are removed; a screen-reader status preserves the selected definition, source and candidate qualifications. Other pages keep their existing group choices. Candidate rows are counted but excluded from the graph. The cached detail must agree with membership identity, provenance and selected-member status. Request results are scoped to the current selection and obsolete effects cannot update it. Context markers outside filters are separate from the filtered Atlas sample and statistics. Four native layers reuse groupTidalEdges and the same sign semantics as Group Page. Atlas marker positions are used for sky projection. No physical boundness claim is made. Existing individual MD context is retained when no resolved group is available or the request fails (labelled unavailable).

Stage 04b: per-edge hover/click tooltip, root/selected marker emphasis and browser acceptance including fast marker changes, physical membership and MD fallback, RA wrap, zoom/pan, filtering, mobile/fullscreen. No claim of complete #64 until those checks pass.
